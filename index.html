All your Google mail accounts in one Mac app.

MailHub is an email app for macOS that brings many Gmail and Google Workspace accounts into one combined inbox. It looks and works the way people already use mail in the browser, and every reply goes out from the address that received the email, with that address's signature.

For macOS 14 and later, with an iPhone app on the way. Your mail stays on your devices: MailHub never stores your mail on a server.

What it does

One inbox for every account

Add as many Google accounts as you use. See them together in All inboxes, or one account at a time, each with its own colour.

Replies from the right address

MailHub picks the address the email was sent to (To, then Cc, then the delivery headers) and adds that address's signature. You can still change it.

Familiar and fast

Conversation view, labels, stars, archive, search with the usual operators, and the keyboard shortcuts you already know. Recent mail is cached so it opens instantly and works offline.

Signatures per address

Signatures are imported from each account's send-as settings and can be edited in MailHub. Changing From swaps the signature automatically.

Everything stays in sync

Archive, read, star, label, delete and send in MailHub and the change is made in your Google account, so your phone and the web see it too.

Notifications per account

Choose which accounts notify you about new mail. The Dock badge counts unread conversations in the accounts you include.

Privacy in short

  • You sign in on Google's own page. MailHub never sees your password.
  • Your sign-in is kept in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. Your mail and settings are kept in MailHub's own storage on your Mac.
  • MailHub talks to Google's APIs for your mail. The only MailHub server is a small notification relay for the iPhone app, which never sees your mail. There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Removing an account in MailHub deletes its mail from your Mac and signs MailHub out of it.

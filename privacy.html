MailHub

Privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

MailHub is an email app for macOS that lets you read, organise and send mail from your Google accounts (Gmail and Google Workspace) in one place. This policy explains what data MailHub uses, where it is kept and how you can remove it.

The short version: MailHub runs entirely on your Mac. Your mail, your sign-in and your settings stay on your Mac. MailHub has no server, collects no analytics and shows no ads. The only service MailHub exchanges your data with is Google, to do what you ask (the messages you send then go, through Google, to the people you write to).

1. Data MailHub accesses

When you add a Google account and allow access, MailHub uses Google's APIs to access:

  • Your email address, name and Google account identity, to know which account you added.
  • Your Gmail messages and conversations (headers, bodies and attachments), labels, drafts, send-as addresses and their signatures, so it can show, sync, organise and send your mail.
  • Your Google Contacts, your "Other contacts" and, for Google Workspace accounts, your organisation's directory, only to suggest recipients while you type an address.

2. How each Google permission is used

3. Where your data is kept

  • Sign-in. You sign in on Google's own sign-in page; MailHub never sees or stores your password. Google gives MailHub a long-lived sign-in token, which is stored only in the macOS Keychain on your Mac (one item per account, service com.bhargava.mailhub.google-refresh-token). Short-lived access tokens are kept in memory only.
  • Mail cache. To open instantly and work offline, MailHub keeps a copy of recent mail, labels, signatures, recipient addresses seen in your mail, and your MailHub settings in a database inside its own sandboxed storage on your Mac (~/Library/Containers/com.bhargava.mailhub). Attachments you open are cached there too.
  • Files you save. When you download or save an attachment, it is written where you choose (Downloads by default).
  • Notifications for new mail are shown by macOS on your Mac.

None of this is uploaded anywhere by MailHub. MailHub does not have a server and does not keep any copy of your data outside your Mac.

4. Who receives your data

  • Google. MailHub connects only to Google's sign-in and API services (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com, gmail.googleapis.com, people.googleapis.com) to fetch your mail and apply the actions you take. Google's handling of your data is covered by Google's own privacy policy.
  • The people you email receive the messages you send, as with any email app.
  • Senders of emails with images. Like most email apps, when you open a message that contains images hosted on the web, those images are loaded from the sender's servers, which can tell that the message was opened. Links you click open in your web browser.
  • Nobody else. MailHub does not sell, rent, share or transfer your data, and does not use any analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting service.

5. Limited Use of Google user data

MailHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve MailHub's user-facing email features described above.
  • It is not transferred to anyone, except to Google to carry out your requests, or as required by law.
  • It is not used for advertising, including personalised or retargeted ads.
  • No person reads your data. Your data never leaves your Mac except to go to Google, so the developer has no access to it.
  • It is not used to build or train AI or machine learning models.

6. Keeping and removing your data

  • Remove an account in MailHub (Settings, Accounts, the delete button): MailHub revokes its access with Google, deletes the account's Keychain item and deletes the account's cached mail, attachments and settings from your Mac. Nothing changes in the Google account itself. Email addresses MailHub has learned for recipient suggestions are shared by all accounts and stay until you delete MailHub's data as described below.
  • Delete the app. To remove everything, first remove each account in MailHub, then delete MailHub from Applications. If you delete the app without removing accounts, also delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.bhargava.mailhub and the MailHub items in the Keychain Access app (shown as "MailHub" followed by the email address).
  • Revoke access at Google at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. MailHub then stops syncing that account and asks you to sign in again; the mail already on your Mac stays there until you remove the account.

Cached data is kept on your Mac for as long as the account is in MailHub.

7. Security

MailHub runs in the macOS App Sandbox, signs in with OAuth 2.0 and PKCE, keeps sign-in tokens in the macOS Keychain, talks to Google only over encrypted HTTPS connections, and shows email content in a restricted view that cannot run scripts.

8. Children

MailHub is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
